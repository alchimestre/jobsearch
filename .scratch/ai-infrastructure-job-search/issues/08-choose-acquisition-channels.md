## Answer

### Channel mix (time allocation)

- **Hiring-manager/founder outreach: 60%.** Direct contact where the transition narrative is controlled before a recruiter filter applies keyword matching. Uses the outreach templates from ticket 07.
- **Targeted applications via company ATS: 25%.** Only to roles that pass the employer criteria from ticket 05. Not mass applications — each is filtered and deliberate.
- **Recruiter relationships: 10%.** Inbound pre-qualified with the five mandatory questions (ticket 05). Proactive contact with in-house recruiters at target companies. Cultivate 3–5 specialist tech recruiter relationships in Switzerland and Berlin.
- **Public technical evidence + follow-up: 5%.** GitHub repo linked from CV, LinkedIn, and outreach messages. Follow-up on every contact after 5–7 business days.

### Outreach platform (market-dependent)

- **Switzerland: email-first.** Small teams at CSCS, Lyceum, Loki, IBM Research — direct culture, more personal.
- **Berlin: LinkedIn-first.** Startup ecosystem is LinkedIn-active (Helsing, Zalando, Inceptive, Zeta Global).
- **Fallback:** whichever platform the hiring manager is more reachable on.

### Recruiter strategy

- **Inbound:** respond using the five pre-qualification questions from ticket 05 before investing time in a screen.
- **Outbound:** proactively contact in-house recruiters at target companies (not agency recruiters). Share criteria (tier, seniority, comp floors, remote-start requirement) so they pre-filter.
- **Cultivate 3–5 specialist tech recruiters** in Switzerland and Berlin who place AI-infrastructure roles.

### Application rules

- Only apply via ATS after the role passes the employer criteria (ticket 05).
- Use the outreach message templates from the evidence package (ticket 07).
- Pre-qualify with the five recruiter-screen questions before any screen.
- Track every application, outreach, and response in a simple pipeline (spreadsheet or similar).

### Follow-up rules

- First follow-up after 5–7 business days if no response to outreach.
- Second follow-up after 10–14 business days, then stop.
- One follow-up after recruiter screen if rejected, asking for specific feedback on the qualification mismatch.

### Public evidence

- GitHub repo (gpu-fleet-operator) linked from CV, LinkedIn, and outreach messages.
- Update LinkedIn "About" section with the transition narrative and "currently building" status.
- The repo becomes stronger evidence as the training plan progresses (Python phase by week 6, Go port by week 12).

### What this rules out

- Agency recruiters as a cultivated channel (in-house only).
- Job board mass applications (no Indeed, StepStone, etc.).
- Networking events as a primary channel (too slow for 12-week timeline, kept as opportunistic).
- Open-source contributions as a primary channel (time-intensive, kept as opportunistic if the GPU operator project naturally leads to one).
