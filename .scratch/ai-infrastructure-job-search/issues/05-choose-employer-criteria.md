## Answer

### Admissibility tiers

- **Tier A — AI-infrastructure roles:** The role directly owns AI workload infrastructure (compute, serving, training platform, observability for AI systems). Admit and rank highest.
- **Tier B — SRE/platform at AI-native companies:** The role is SRE/platform work at a company whose core product is AI, even without direct AI-infrastructure ownership. Admit; rank below Tier A.
- **Tier C — High-quality SRE bridge roles:** SRE/platform roles at non-AI companies that offer architectural scope, strong compensation, and a credible path toward AI exposure. Admit only above the bridge compensation floor.

### Rejection criteria (hard filters)

- Roles without AI workload exposure and without architectural scope (generic SRE/DevOps runbooks).
- Crypto-continuity roles that do not strengthen AI-infrastructure positioning.
- Staff/Principal/senior-staff roles asking 8+ years (seniority mismatch).
- French CDI roles below €70k total.
- Switzerland roles below CHF 110k total.
- Berlin roles below €80k total.
- On-site Switzerland or Berlin roles that do not offer either relocation assistance or a remote-start-from-France arrangement for 8–12 weeks.
- Roles requiring direct GPU, training-framework, or serving-engine expertise as day-one requirements (evidence gap not yet closed).

### Ranking criteria (applied within admitted tiers, in priority order)

1. **AI-infrastructure depth:** Does the role own AI workload infrastructure? (Tier A > B > C)
2. **Role scope:** Architectural scope and cross-stakeholder communication over pure operational runbooks.
3. **Seniority match:** Mid to Senior (3–6 years asked). Skip Staff/Principal.
4. **Location and remote-start feasibility:** On-site/hybrid in Zurich, Berlin, or surrounding metro. EU-remote from Switzerland or Germany. France on-site above bridge floor if AI-native or architectural. Any qualifying role must permit remote start from France for 8–12 weeks unless relocation assistance is provided.
5. **Compensation:** Above market-specific floor (Switzerland CHF 110k; Berlin €80k; bridge €70k).
6. **Language:** English-first employers or German-not-required postings. French is an asset in Romandie.
7. **Company stage:** All stages admitted; no stage filter. Ranked by role fit, not size.
8. **Bridge-opportunity value:** A bridge role must materially strengthen AI-infrastructure positioning or enable later relocation. Rank by strategic value, not just compensation.

### Mandatory recruiter-screen questions

These are derived from the pipeline diagnosis (ticket 01) and must be confirmed before investing time in a process:

1. Does the role require direct GPU, training-framework, or serving-engine expertise on day one?
2. What is the seniority band and years-of-experience expectation?
3. What is the total compensation range for this role and location?
4. Is remote start from France for 8–12 weeks possible, or is relocation assistance provided?
5. What is the team's current AI workload stack and infrastructure maturity?
What explicit criteria should admit, rank, or reject employers and roles across Swiss and Berlin markets, including AI-infrastructure depth, role scope, seniority, location feasibility, language, compensation, company stage, and bridge-opportunity value?
