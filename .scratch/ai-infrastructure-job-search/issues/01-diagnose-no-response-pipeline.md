# Diagnose the No-Response Pipeline

Type: grilling
Status: resolved

## Question

Where is the current job-search funnel actually failing—role selection, eligibility, positioning, application assets, channel choice, credibility signals, or follow-up—and what evidence distinguishes the primary bottleneck from secondary problems?

## Answer

The observed failure is not lack of application response. During the most recent active eight-week period, 11–30 applications produced at least four positive human responses, a lower-bound screen rate of 13%. The recurring drop-off occurred after recruiter screens and before technical interviews.

The screened sample mixed general SRE and crypto roles rather than testing the intended AI-infrastructure position. Aggregate conversion therefore cannot establish whether AI-infrastructure targeting works. Three distinct recruiter-screen filters appeared:

1. **Relevance translation:** employers did not consistently see prior crypto SRE work as relevant to their immediate domain problem.
2. **Immediate-readiness evidence:** some opportunities required direct experience that would let a hire contribute without a substantial ramp-up; adjacent infrastructure capability was insufficient.
3. **Compensation fit:** some French CDI roles capped compensation around €50k, below the candidate’s acceptable range. A stated expectation around €100k total for London or Switzerland may also have screened out some roles, but combining those markets cannot establish whether that expectation is miscalibrated in either one.

Primary decision: treat this as a **recruiter-screen qualification problem**, not a response-volume problem. The strategy must pre-qualify role level, required direct experience, market-specific compensation, and location before applying; then make the SRE-to-AI-infrastructure relevance case explicit. Do not infer that the existing CV is incapable of generating interest. Establish AI-infrastructure conversion separately from legacy SRE/crypto conversion.
