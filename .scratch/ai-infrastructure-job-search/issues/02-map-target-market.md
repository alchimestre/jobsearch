# Map the Switzerland and Berlin AI Infrastructure Market

Type: research
Status: resolved

## Question

Which employer segments, companies, role-title aliases, hiring locations, language expectations, and recurring requirements define the realistic AI Infrastructure Engineer market in Switzerland and Berlin, using the agreed 60/40 priority and tiered employer thesis?

## Answer

Switzerland’s realistic funnel is Zurich-centered: international and ETH-domain infrastructure employers, selective model-lab roles, and a small GPU-platform startup segment. Berlin’s stronger lane is in-house ML platform/MLOps at AI product companies plus infrastructure-heavy AI scale-ups. English-first roles are viable in both markets; domestic Swiss employers may add German requirements. French EU citizenship removes labor-market admission barriers, but Switzerland still requires the applicable cantonal L/B EU/EFTA residence-permit process for employment beyond three months, unlike Germany’s no-residence-permit rule.

Search by responsibility across `AI Infrastructure`, `AI/ML Platform`, `GPU Platform`, `SRE — ML/Inference`, `Training Platform`, `Inference Platform`, and `HPC/AI Platform` aliases. Preserve the 60% Switzerland / 40% Berlin allocation, but map the employer tiers differently by market: Zurich carries most infrastructure-vendor/global-compute targets; Berlin carries more internal ML-platform targets.
