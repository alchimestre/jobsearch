- **No claim of customer-facing experience.** This is the known gap (`docs/resume-edits.md`, open since
  2026-09-11, unresolved at drafting time). The draft offers *on-premises deployment substance* — the
  capability behind the customer-facing requirement — without claiming customer-facing history. **If Vincent
  supplies a concrete customer-facing situation (external audit at Cometh's ISO 27001 work, HDS
  certification engagement, institutional client work at Kiln, external incident bridges), it belongs in
  paragraph two and should be added before sending.**
- **Does not mention compensation.** Cohere has not quoted a band.
- **Closes on a direct question**, which forces a reply.
- **Does not name the tier or the cap** — internal tracking language has no place in applicant copy.

**Do not repeat the mistake that created this.** Cohere was applied to twice without a contact route, in a

and per the playbook, one-shot ATS applications are the 25% lane, not the 60% lane.
