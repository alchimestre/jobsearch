## What the role is

WeScale is a ~70-person French collective doing Platform Engineering, Cloud, DevOps and AI. The role
builds and runs the platform of the company's "Augmented Software Factory": CI/CD pipelines with agents,
cloud-native infrastructure via IaC, end-to-end observability including over AI outputs (drift, latency,
quality), and reusable infrastructure blueprints across clients. Delivery-facing, not a separate ops silo.
Career path Engineer → Senior → Lead → Staff → Principal. Budget **€75,000–€90,000** gross, 25 days +
11 RTT, Swile, 50% transport, Wellpass.

## Fit

The stack is the CV, item for item: Kubernetes/Helm/Kustomize, GitHub Actions and GitLab CI, ArgoCD or
Flux, Terraform first, Prometheus/Grafana/Loki/OpenTelemetry with SLOs and error budgets, DevSecOps,
secrets management. Two requirements are the real risk:

1. **"8+ ans d'expérience"** against ~4 years of evidenced continuous infrastructure roles
   (Dec 2021 → Jan 2026). This is the leading elimination risk and the reason the tier is C rather than
   a clean fit. Do not inflate the tenure; lead with scope ($8B+ secured across 40+ protocol networks,
   multi-zone Kubernetes on GCP, HDS-certified healthcare).
2. **"Expérience en conseil"** — consultancy delivery. The CV's employer history is not a consultancy
   trail; answer it with client-facing delivery and handover work if asked, not with a claim.

What eases it: the JD asks for curiosity about AI in pipelines, explicitly not prior ML-platform
ownership. Non-determinism, cost and output observability are the framing, and that framing is reachable
from the SRE record.

## Screen prep

- **Salary:** "€90,000, haut de la fourchette annoncée. La fourchette basse à 75k est sous ce que je peux
  considérer, la responsabilité du poste (blueprints réutilisables, observabilité des sorties IA) est au
  niveau staff."
- **Tenure:** four years of continuous production infrastructure, with the scope usually attached to
  longer careers — cite the assets secured, the protocol count, the zones, the certifications.
- **Consultancy:** how much of the role is client mission vs internal platform; whether the blueprint work
  is billable delivery or internal tooling.
- **On-call and travel:** client-site presence in Île-de-France, on-call rotation and its compensation.
- **AI in the loop:** which parts of the delivery pipeline are actually agent-generated today, and what
  guardrails exist. 95 applicants after one day, so the screen is not guaranteed.
