## What the role is

Mayday sells AI-augmented knowledge management to 150 enterprise customers (Fnac Darty, EDF, Cdiscount,
Doctolib, Qonto, URSSAF), raised $3M, and now sits inside the USU group. One Senior DevOps Engineer owns
the multi-cloud (Azure + AWS), multi-region (EU + USA) SaaS platform, reporting to co-founder Hugo. Three
blocks: infrastructure and Kubernetes run/evolve; reliability, security and disaster recovery; and
enablement of the Engineering, Product and AI teams, including the agentic-engineering dev environment.
Stack: Azure+AWS, Docker/Kubernetes, ArgoCD, Terraform, GitLab CI, Datadog, PostgreSQL/MongoDB/Elasticsearch/
Redis, RabbitMQ, Temporal, TypeScript/Python, Claude Code. Salary **€75,000–€85,000**, start stated as
**2026-11-02**, partial remote, Bac+5, 5+ years.

   reporting line, plus the migration work that is inherently cloud-agnostic.

Bonus items land well: multi-region, disaster-recovery design, DevSecOps and enterprise security
questionnaires (Docaposte HDS-certified healthcare), FinOps (25% cloud-cost reduction through
architecture changes). None of them is a prerequisite.
